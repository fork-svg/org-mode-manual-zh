发布

Org 支持将项目发布为网站或文档集合。

发布项目

发布项目定义了一组文件和导出设置：

(setq org-publish-project-alist
      '(("org-notes"
         :base-directory "~/Documents/org/"
         :base-extension "org"
         :publishing-directory "~/public_html/"
         :publishing-function org-html-publish-to-html
         :with-section-numbers t
         :with-toc t
         :html-head "<link rel=\"stylesheet\" href=\"style.css\" type=\"text/css\"/>"
         :auto-sitemap t
         :sitemap-filename "index.org"
         :sitemap-title "我的笔记")

        ("org-static"
         :base-directory "~/Documents/org/"
         :base-extension "css\\|js\\|png\\|jpg\\|gif\\|pdf"
         :publishing-directory "~/public_html/"
         :publishing-function org-publish-attachment)

        ("website" :components ("org-notes" "org-static"))))

发布命令

快捷键功能
C-c C-e P x发布项目
C-c C-e P p发布当前文件
C-c C-e P f强制发布当前文件
C-c C-e P s发布当前项目
C-c C-e P S强制发布当前项目
C-c C-e P b发布所有项目
C-c C-e P B强制发布所有项目

项目属性

基本属性

  • :base-directory：源文件目录
  • :base-extension：文件扩展名
  • :publishing-directory：发布目标目录
  • :publishing-function：发布函数

常用发布函数

  • org-html-publish-to-html：导出为 HTML
  • org-latex-publish-to-pdf：导出为 PDF
  • org-latex-publish-to-latex：导出为 LaTeX
  • org-publish-attachment：复制附件文件

内容属性

  • :with-toc：是否生成目录
  • :section-numbers：是否编号
  • :with-author：是否包含作者
  • :html-head：自定义 HTML head

站点地图

  • :auto-sitemap：自动生成站点地图
  • :sitemap-filename：站点地图文件名
  • :sitemap-title：站点地图标题
  • :sitemap-sort-folders：文件夹排序方式

示例：简单网站

(setq org-publish-project-alist
      '(("blog"
         :base-directory "~/blog/"
         :publishing-directory "~/public_html/"
         :recursive t
         :publishing-function org-html-publish-to-html
         :html-head "<link rel=\"stylesheet\" href=\"/css/style.css\" type=\"text/css\"/>"
         :html-postamble nil
         :auto-sitemap t
         :sitemap-title "我的博客"
         :sitemap-sort-folders last)))

示例：复杂项目

(setq org-publish-project-alist
      '(("org-content"
         :base-directory "~/projects/docs/"
         :base-extension "org"
         :publishing-directory "~/projects/public/"
         :publishing-function org-html-publish-to-html
         :with-toc t
         :html-head "<link rel=\"stylesheet\" href=\"css/main.css\" type=\"text/css\"/>")

        ("org-images"
         :base-directory "~/projects/docs/images/"
         :base-extension "jpg\\|png\\|gif\\|svg"
         :publishing-directory "~/projects/public/images/"
         :publishing-function org-publish-attachment)

        ("org-css"
         :base-directory "~/projects/docs/css/"
         :base-extension "css"
         :publishing-directory "~/projects/public/css/"
         :publishing-function org-publish-attachment)

        ("docs" :components ("org-content" "org-images" "org-css"))))

同步

发布时使用 Rsync 同步：

(setq org-publish-project-alist
      '(("remote"
         :base-directory "~/docs/"
         :publishing-function org-html-publish-to-html
         :publishing-function (lambda (plist filename)
                                (let ((html (org-html-publish-to-html plist filename)))
                                  (call-process "rsync" nil nil nil "-avz"
                                                html "user@server:/var/www/")))))))
